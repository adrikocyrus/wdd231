Website Plan

Kampala Chamber of Commerce — prepared by Adriko Cyrus

Site Name

Kampala Chamber of Commerce

The name tells visitors right away that the site serves businesses in Kampala, Uganda, and it matches how the organization would be recognized and searched for locally.

Site Purpose

The site is a hub that connects local businesses, events, and opportunities in Kampala. It provides a member directory that visitors can browse, current chamber news and events, and a membership application form with details on the NP, Bronze, Silver, and Gold levels.

Scenarios

  • How can I find a trusted local business in a particular industry?
  • What does it cost to become a member, and what benefits do I get at each level?
  • What chamber events are coming up, and how can I attend?
  • How do I contact the chamber's office?

Color Scheme

These colors are used on this document and are planned for the full site.

  • Primary green #14532d
    Headings, header and footer backgrounds, links, borders.
  • Accent gold #f5b700
    Accent stripes, card borders, button hover states.
  • Text charcoal #1f2933
    Paragraph and list text.
  • Background cream #fdfaf3
    Page background.
  • White #ffffff
    Section and card backgrounds, text on green.

Typography

Poppins (600 and 700)

Used for the site name, page headings, and subheadings.

Open Sans (400 and 600)

Used for body text, navigation, forms, and lists because it stays easy to read at small sizes.

Wireframe

Black and white sketches of the home page for small and large screens.
